# 🎮 Multiplayer Quick Start Guide

## What's New?

Your Simon game now has **turn-based multiplayer** for 2-4 players!

---

## 🚀 Quick Start

### Local Multiplayer (Same Device)
```
1. Play with friends at home
2. Each player takes turns
3. Longer sequence = harder game
4. First player to beat the sequence wins the round!
```

**Steps**:
1. Click **"Multiplayer"**
2. Select **"Local Multiplayer (2-4 Players)"**
3. Choose **2, 3, or 4 players**
4. Take turns clicking squares!

---

### Online Multiplayer (Network)
```
1. Play with friends online
2. Create or join a room
3. All players see real-time scores
4. Turn-based gameplay ensures fairness
```

**Steps**:
1. Click **"Multiplayer"**
2. Select **"Online Multiplayer (Turn-Based)"**
3. Create room or join existing room
4. Wait for players to join (max 4)
5. Click **"Start Game"**
6. Take turns as the sequence grows!

---

## 📊 Scoring

- **+1 point** = Complete one sequence successfully
- **Mistake** = End of turn, next player goes
- **Winner** = Highest score at end of game

---

```

```

## 💡 Tips

1. **Watch the pattern** - Focus on the sequence
2. **Take your time** - No rush, click carefully
3. **Watch others** - See if they make mistakes (local)
4. **Keep score** - Remember your best round!

---

## ❌ Mistakes End Turns

- One wrong click = your turn ends
- Next player starts fresh with new sequence
- Scores don't reset between turns
- Keep playing until you decide to stop!
